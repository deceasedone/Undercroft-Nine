ABOUT & INFO

HOW IT WORKS

  • Chain shards within 3 s to raise the multiplier (up to ×8). Getting hit resets it.
  • Recover every data node [R], then fly into the core to clear the sector. Every 5th sector the Warden shields the core.
  • Sentinels shoot when they can see you; searchlights mark you so nearby sentinels converge. Dash [Q] passes through shots.
  • Threat rises every 5 minutes of flight: faster enemies, more fire, reinforcements.

DAILY RUN

Everyone gets the same city layouts for the day (a new city at midnight UTC — today: ). Play it as often as you like; use COPY RESULT after a run to share your score.

YOUR PROGRESS

Archive fragments, top runs and settings are saved only in this browser, for this exact web address. Nothing is sent anywhere. They don't expire, but are lost if you clear site data or play in a private window; Safari may remove them after about 7 days without a visit.
